Privacy Policy

Rewire (iOS and Android) · Last updated: November 12, 2026

In short: Rewire does not collect any personal data, and does not send it to a server, sell it or share it. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. Everything stays on your device.

What does Rewire store?

To work, Rewire stores the following only on your device, in an area shared between the app and its system extensions:

  • Your block list. iOS gives this list to Rewire only as anonymous identifiers. Rewire cannot see which app or website these identifiers belong to.
  • Your schedules, limits and settings.
  • Simple counters, such as completed focus sessions, streaks and how many times you closed a blocked app.
  • Your answers from the welcome screens (hours per day, how much you want back, and what for) and your progress in the 21-day program.
  • On Android, each day's total screen time (in minutes, for up to 400 days), so the brain calendar can be shown. How long you spent in which app is not stored.

Because this information never leaves your device, it never reaches us; we can't see it and can't give it to anyone.

Screen Time data (iOS)

Rewire uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). The usage reports in the Stats tab are generated by iOS inside a privacy-protected extension. This data cannot leave that extension. Neither the Rewire app, nor we, nor anyone else can access it.

Internet access

Rewire has no server, and the app makes no network requests of its own. It contains no analytics tools, ad networks or crash reporters. It talks to the store app on your device (the App Store or Google Play) for two things only: purchases and the store's own review prompt. Rewire sends the store no information about you when it does.

Notifications

If you allow it, Rewire reminds you of the day's task, tells you when a session ends or when your limit is about to run out. These are local notifications created on your device: they are not sent from a server, no push token is collected, and nothing is recorded anywhere. You can turn them off at any time under Settings → Notifications in the app, or in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Rewire Pro is a one-time purchase. Payment is handled by Apple on iOS and by Google Play on Android. Rewire never sees your card details, name or email address. The app only learns whether Pro is unlocked and stores that on your device.

Android

On Android, blocking and stats rely on different system permissions than on iOS. You turn both on yourself in Settings, and you can turn them off at any time.

  • Accessibility Service (for blocking, required). Rewire uses this service only to learn which app is opened on screen (its package name). If the app is on your block list, Rewire shows the block screen. The service is configured so that it cannot read screen content: it does not read what you type, your messages, your passwords or anything else you see on screen. The name of the opened app is not stored and is never sent anywhere. Unlike on iOS, your block list is stored as the package names of the apps you chose; this list also stays only on your device. The service is used only for blocking, never for advertising, analytics or any other purpose.
  • Usage access (for stats and the daily limit, optional). If you grant this permission, Rewire reads Android's usage statistics to show your screen time, pickups and top time-taking apps, and to enforce the daily limit. This data is processed only on your device.
  • App list. So you can build your block list, Rewire lists only the apps that appear on your home screen. It does not request access to every package on your device.
  • No internet permission. The Android version has no internet permission, so data cannot leave your device. Purchases are made through the Play Store app on your device.
  • No backups. Rewire's data is not backed up to the cloud and is not transferred to other devices.

Deleting your data

You can use Settings → Delete all data in the app, or delete the app. To revoke Screen Time access, go to iOS Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access. On Android, you can turn the permissions off under Settings → Accessibility and Settings → Usage access (menu names may vary slightly by manufacturer).

Children

Rewire is designed for the person using the device to manage their own habits; it is not directed at children and is not a parental control tool. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Health

"Brain health", "brain battery" and "rot" are a visual metaphor that compares your screen time with your daily goal. They are not a medical measurement, diagnosis or treatment.

If you write to us (GDPR and KVKK)

Because we collect no personal data through the app, we hold no record about you. The one exception is if you email us: we then process your email address and what you wrote, only so we can reply (legal basis: necessary to handle your request, and legitimate interest). We don't share this correspondence, don't use it for marketing, and delete it within one year of the matter being closed. Emails are stored on our email provider's servers, which may be located outside your country.

The data controller is the developer named on the app's store page. To exercise your rights under the GDPR or Turkey's Law No. 6698 (KVKK), for example to have your correspondence deleted, just write to the address below; we reply within 30 days. If our answer doesn't satisfy you, you can lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
