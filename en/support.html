Help & Support

iPhone

An app I want to block isn't being blocked

Make sure Rewire has Screen Time access (iOS Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access). Also check that the app is on your block list. iOS can block up to 50 individual apps. For more, select the whole category.

A block didn't lift on time

iOS decides when Rewire runs in the background. Every time you open Rewire, the status is checked again and expired blocks are removed.

I can't turn off Strict Mode

That's on purpose. While a session, schedule or daily limit is running, Strict Mode only lets you make your rules stricter. The lock opens as soon as the block ends.

Android

No apps are being blocked

On Android, blocking works through Rewire's Accessibility service. Check that the service is on under Settings → Accessibility → Installed apps → Rewire. The Settings → Permissions section in Rewire also shows the service's status. Also make sure the app is on your block list.

Rewire is grayed out in Accessibility settings

If you installed the app outside the Play Store, Android restricts this permission. Go to Settings → Apps → Rewire → the ⋮ menu in the top right → "Allow restricted settings", then go back to Accessibility and turn Rewire on.

Blocking stops working after a while

Some phones shut down background services to save battery. Set Rewire to "Unrestricted" under Settings → Apps → Rewire → Battery. After restarting your phone, check that the Accessibility service is still on. Menu names may vary slightly by manufacturer.

Stats are empty and the daily limit doesn't work

Screen time stats and the daily limit need the Usage access permission. In Rewire, tap Settings → Permissions → Usage access and turn Rewire on in the list.

The block showed up a few seconds late

If you're inside a blocked app when a schedule starts or your break ends, Rewire notices within a few seconds and shows the block screen. The daily limit is checked against usage data, so it can take a little longer to kick in.

I can delete Rewire while Strict Mode is on

Android doesn't let apps protect themselves from being uninstalled; that's only possible on iPhone. On Android, Strict Mode turns off early exits, breaks and loosening your rules.

General

Pro is missing on my new phone

In Rewire, tap Settings → Restore purchase. Pro is tied to the Apple Account or Google account you bought it with, so you need to be signed in with the same account on your new phone.

I want a refund for my purchase

Apple or Google handles the payment, so they also handle refunds. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, use your order history in Google Play.

Is my data safe?

Rewire has no server, and the app makes no network requests of its own. The Android version doesn't even have internet permission. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I turn off notifications?

In Rewire, go to Settings → Notifications to turn off each of the four kinds separately. Notifications are created on your device; they don't come from a server.

Is "brain health" a medical measurement?

No. It's a visual metaphor that compares your screen time with your daily goal; it is not a diagnosis or a treatment. If you're worried about your health, talk to a doctor.
